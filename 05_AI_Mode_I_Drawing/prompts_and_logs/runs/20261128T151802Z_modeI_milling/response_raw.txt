## 3. Toolpath assumptions
- **Safe heights.** All XY rapids are at Z10. Rapid descent stops at Z1, and every move below Z1 is fed at F60, even where the material is already removed.
- **Tool changes.** Each change follows the same sequence: Z10, M05, G49, Tnn M06, S4000 M03, G00 G43 Hnn Z10. G40 and G80 are modal from the header.
- **Compensation.** There is no G41/G42; tool-centre paths are computed from T02 R5 and T03 R3. G43 Hnn is used with nn = tool number.
- **OP10 (T02), one 4 mm level.** This is the approved maximum for general removal.
  - Zigzag strips enter from outside the block at X-7 or X177. Stepover is 6 to 6.75 mm.
  - The T02 centre stays outside a square |X-40|, |Y-40| ≤ 30.25. That keeps it at least 7.25 mm from every pin centre (1.75 pin + 5 tool + 0.5 clearance).
  - The material left inside the square is then removed by:
    - a circle at tool-centre R30.75 (covers r 25.75–35.75, which includes the square corners);
    - a slot along Y40 between pins 1 and 6 (11.5 mm from each pin axis);
    - circles at R8 and R15.75 (covers r ≤ 20.75).
  - Only the ring r 20.75–25.75 around the pins remains for OP30.
  - Because the whole block is faced to Z-4, the small boss top face is produced wherever the boss ends up, so OP10 does not depend on d.
- **OP20 bore D30 (T02).**
  - At each level the tool plunges at the bore centre (removes r < 5), moves out to tool-centre R9.8, does a full CCW circle (climb), and returns to centre.
  - Levels are 2 mm because radial engagement is full width.
  - Finish is one CCW circle at R10 over the full depth: 0.2 mm radial engagement, with the 25 mm flute covering Z-24.5 to Z+0.5.
- **OP30 pins (T03, 2 levels of 2 mm).** Each pin gets two full CW (climb) loops per level:
  - a loop of radius 10 about the pin axis, clearing 7–13 mm from the axis;
  - a loop of radius 4.75, finishing the D3.5 pin.
  - Entry and exit are radially outward at r33 and r27.75 from the boss axis, which is already cleared air.
  - The farthest leftover point midway between two pins is 12.9 mm from the nearest pin axis, so it is covered.
  - The closest approach to a neighbouring pin is 10 mm.
- **OP40 keyway (T03).**
  - CCW (climb) rectangle with tool centre X39–41, Y50–55, in 2 mm levels from Z-6 to Z-24.5.
  - Z moves happen inside the D30 bore void, at most 13.05 mm from the axis, which is less than 15.
  - The result is width 8, bottom flat at Y58 (18 from the axis), with R3 bottom corners instead of sharp (see OP45).
  - The tool stays 3.25 mm clear of pin 2.
- **Why these tools.**
  - T02 and T03 are the only end mills with approved data.
  - D10 suits the D30 and D20 bores and open areas.
  - D6 is under the keyway width of 8 and fits around the D3.5 pins.
  - D6 (R3) can make the R8 window corners later.
  - T10 (corner R2) is the only tool that can generate the R2 fillets.
  - The maximum depth of 24.5 is within the 25 mm flute length of T02 and T03, but only by 0.5 mm.

## 5. Uncertainty and limitations

**Overall status:** only OP10–OP40 have NC code. Every other operation depends on dimensions the drawing does not give. Once the team supplies the boss centre distance and confirms the arm height position and arc tangency, OP50–OP120 can be generated.

**G1 – geometry gate**
- **Boss centre distance.** It is missing from every view, so the small boss, the D20 bore, both arms, the window and all of S2 are blocked. Please supply it from the CAD revision "Ch2-Final" or a corrected drawing.
- **Arm edges.** Confirm that R115 is internally tangent and R60 externally tangent to both bosses, and that the arm inner edges are concentric offsets (R107, R68). The formulas are in the operation-plan assumptions.
- **Stock fit.** Once the centre distance is known, check that the part fits the stock: small boss at X40 + d + 21 ≤ 170, and rim apex Y ≤ 85.
- **Arm height.** Confirm the 10-thick arms are centred in the 20 boss height (S1 Z-9 to Z-19).
- **Pins.**
  - Confirm six pins, equally spaced at 60 deg, one on +Y, on radius 23.
  - Confirm the reading of 23 and 18 in B-B.
  - Confirm the pins are integral and not inserted dowels.
- **Keyway.** Sharp corners are drawn, but milling leaves R3. Is OP45 (broaching or slotting) required? A width tolerance is also missing.
- **Fillets and title block.** Confirm the extent of the R2 fillets and of the plan-view blends. Tolerances, surface finish and material are blank on the drawing.
- **Z0.** Confirm Z0 = pin tops, which relies on 20 + 4 = 24 = stock height.

**G2 – process gate**
- **Facing with an end mill.** Facing 170 × 85 at 4 mm depth with T02 is slow but uses only approved data. A face mill (T01) would need approved cutting data.
- **Bore entry.** OP20 plunges a D10 end mill at 60 mm/min, so T02 must be centre-cutting. The alternative is helical entry, but MillLab support for helical G02/G03 with Z is unverified.
- **Breakthrough.** The 0.5 mm breakthrough to Z-24.5 needs fixture relief under the bores and keyway. The real S2 fixture must support the part once the frame is separated.
- **Finishing allowance.** There is none on the pins and keyway (single pass), and only 0.2 mm on the bore. Fit-critical diameters may need a separate finishing pass once tolerances are known.
- **Coolant and deburring.** Coolant is not programmed. Deburring (F13) is manual.

**G3 – code gate**
- **Commands to test in MillLab:**
  - full-circle G02/G03 with the same start and end point;
  - G43 H02/H03 against the tool-table lengths;
  - the tool-change sequence M05/G49/M06;
  - feeds above Z0 in air;
  - whether MillLab flags a vise or base collision at Z-24.5. If it does, use Z-24.0 and accept a possible skin.
- **Flute margin.** The maximum depth of 24.5 against a 25 mm flute (T02, T03) leaves only 0.5 mm. Check for shank contact in the simulator.
- **Clearance checks to re-verify independently:**
  - pin-to-tool clearance in OP10 is 0.5 mm (square edges and the R15.75 circle) and 1.0 mm (the R30.75 circle);
  - the OP30 loops and the OP40 keyway rectangle are within the bore void;
  - the I/J values are rounded to 0.001.
- **Individual op files.** They are not standalone from a raw block. Simulate with setup_01_complete.nc, since MillLab starts from a raw block. S2 will also start from a raw block in MillLab, so its result will not show the S1 features.
