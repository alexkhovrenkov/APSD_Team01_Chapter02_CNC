## 3. Toolpath assumptions
- **Approach and retract.** Every cutting tool rapids to X110 Z2 first (outside the D105 bar, 2 mm in front of the face).
  - Drills (T10, T05) position at X0 Z5, then Z2 on the axis, and retract to Z5.
  - The boring bar goes X110 Z2 → X12 Z2 (inside the D13 pilot), and leaves via Z5 → X110.
  - The threading tool goes X110 Z2 → Z10 → X56 Z10, giving 2 pitches of run-up for synchronisation.
- **Tool changes and rapids.** Before every tool change: M09, G28 U0 W0, M05, then the T call. G00 is used only in air, inside an already drilled hole on the axis, or inside a roughed bore.
- **Facing (T01, G94).** One pass from Z+1 to Z0, across to X0. The centre nub left by the nose radius is removed later by drilling.
- **External roughing (T01, G71 type I).**
  - 2.5 mm radial steps with 0.5 mm retract, leaving U1.0 (0.5 mm per side) and W0.5 on faces.
  - Both profiles are monotonic (X only increases towards the chuck), as type I requires.
  - S1 profile: D50 to Z-125 → G02 R5 → D60 → face up to D100 → D100 to Z-152 → X106 (above stock).
  - S2 profile: D29.9 to Z-20 → face to D60 → D60 to Z-55 → G02 R5 → D70 → face up to X106.
  - The CNMG 80° R0.8 is a strong roughing insert, and its 95° approach angle allows the square shoulders.
- **External finishing (T02, explicit G01/G02 contour).**
  - G42 is switched on with a 60 mm or 70 mm radial move in front of the face, and G40 on the retract to X110.
  - The VNMG 35° R0.4 has enough back clearance to climb the concave R5 fillets and the shoulder faces. Nose radius 0.4 < R5, so the fillets are produced exactly with compensation (tip 3).
  - Arc direction: in the +X up / +Z right picture, both fillets run clockwise (from the cylinder tangent up to the face tangent), so they are programmed as G02.
- **Centre drilling (T10).** 2 mm deep, which gives a spot of about D4 for D13 drill location.
- **Drilling (T05).** Pecks of 10 mm (≈0.8 D) with full retract to Z5 for chip clearing. Re-entry is a rapid to 0.5 mm above the previous depth. Tip depth Z-65, full diameter to about Z-61.1.
- **Internal roughing (T06, G90 ID box cycle).**
  - Start point X12 Z2. Passes X15 → X29 in 2 mm diameter steps (1.0 mm radial), to Z-39.5.
  - Each cycle retracts inward at feed along the bore bottom plane and returns to Z2 inside the D13 pilot (tip at radius 6 < 6.5).
- **Internal finishing (T06).**
  - G41 on (tip 2, ID turning towards the chuck). Wall at D30 to Z-40, then the bottom face inward to X12.5 (inside the D13 hole edge).
  - Feed retract 0.5 mm off the bottom, G40, then rapid out at X12 on the axis side.
  - The D10 bar with 80 mm reach covers the 40 mm depth; the 55° DCMT reaches the square bottom corner.
- **Threading (T04, G76 two-block form).**
  - P020060: 2 finishing (spring) passes, no pull-out chamfer, 60° profile. Q100 = minimum cut 0.1 mm. R0.05 = finishing allowance.
  - Second block: X43.87 Z-122.0, P3067 = thread height 3.067 mm, Q700 = first cut 0.7 mm, F5.0.
  - G97 S150 gives 750 mm/min axial feed, well inside the 12000 mm/min rapid limit.
- **Compensation.** Used only on the finishing contours (OP30, OP70, OP130); G40 is active for all cycles (G71, G90, G94, G76) and drilling.
- **Speeds.** Constant rpm (G97) everywhere, as planned by the team. All speeds ≤ 800 rpm, so far below the 4000 rpm limit; no G50 is needed because G96 is not used.

## 5. Uncertainty and limitations

**Geometry gate G1**
- **Units.** The title block says "DIMENSIONS ARE IN INCHES"; every value here was taken as mm, per the team configuration. Confirm with the drawing author.
- **External thread.** D50 comes from the radial dimension 25 (axis to crest), and pitch 5 from the callout between crests. Designation, class, hand and form are missing. Confirm whether the thread major should be undersized for the thread class.
- **Derived axial positions.** The flange position (S1 Z-130 to Z-150) and the D60 body length (40 including the fillet) are derived, not dimensioned. Check that the 125 dimension starts at the R5 tangent point.
- **Diameter readings.**
  - R15 was read as the bore radius (D30).
  - D29.9 was assigned to the spigot because the right view shows the spigot end in first-angle projection.
  - The radial 23 was read as the radius of the hole pattern (PCD 46).
- **Undefined features.** The internal thread has no size or pitch. The meaning of the "2" and "4.1" callouts is uncertain. Keyway depth, length and orientation are missing, as are chamfers, tolerances and surface finish.

**Process gate G2**
- **Through hole cannot be completed.** With T05 (70 mm flute length), the full-diameter D13 hole reaches only S1 Z-61.1 from the front and S1 Z-148.9 from the rear. About 88 mm in the middle stays undrilled (OP170 blocked). A longer drill or a decision by the team is needed.
- **Internal thread cannot be programmed (OP160).** The thread size is unknown, T09 reach is 15 against 41.42 needed, and a D12 bar cannot work inside a D13 core hole.
- **Thread run-out.** There is no relief groove, so the thread ends at Z-122 (assumption). Threading to Z-125 would gouge the R5 fillet with the tool flank. Team decision: shorter thread, a relief groove, or accept the incomplete last turn.
- **Tool "reach" for the OD tools.** If LatheLab treats reach (15 mm) as a limit on radial engagement or shoulder depth, the following may raise collision or reach warnings. The tools were not changed, per the team decision.
  - The D50 thread major, 27.5 mm radial from D105.
  - The D29.9 spigot, 37.5 mm radial.
  - The D60 body, 22.5 mm radial.
- **Real rear excess length.**
  - After S1 the real part still carries about 29 mm extra at the rear (240 - 210 - 1). S2 is written for the simulated raw bar with only 1 mm face allowance.
  - On a real machine, the approach point X110 Z2 would lie inside this material.
  - The excess must be sawn or parted off first; T07 has no approved cutting data.
- **S2 grip surface.** Not specified. Gripping on the thread damages it, and gripping on the 20 mm wide flange may block access to the flange left face.
- **Bore start.** The minimum bore diameter of T06 is not in the tool table. Check that the first G90 pass at D15 from a D13 pilot is possible without the bar rubbing.
- **Overhang.** In S1 there is 215 mm of stick-out with a D50 section over 125 mm and a 5 mm pitch thread. This is acceptable in simulation, but not credible without support on a real machine.
- **Threading parameters.** The first cut of 0.7 mm and 2 spring passes for P5 in C45 are proposals. Check the pass count (about 19 passes plus 2).

**Code gate G3**
