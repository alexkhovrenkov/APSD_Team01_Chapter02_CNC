## 3. Toolpath assumptions
- **Safe heights.** All XY rapids are at Z10. Each approach is G00 to Z1, then G01 at F60 to depth, and the retract is G00 Z10. At a tool change the program goes to Z10, then M05, G49, Tnn M06, and finally G00 G43 Hnn Z10.
- **Region strategy (S1).**
  - Above the arm, material is removed only inside the part outline: the window pocket, the outer channel, the strip centre-lines (R111 about the R115/R107 centre, R64 about the R60/R68 centre) and the boss rings.
  - The rest of the block stays as a frame.
  - Strip paths end on the boss keep-out circles: r37 for boss 1 (start point (53.7631,74.345) / (76.1756,32.2328)) and r28 for boss 2 (start point (135.7322,66.4218) / (117.5969,34.2489)).
- **Boss walls and fillets.**
  - Wall: T02 at r35 / r26 down to the fillet tangent line (S1 Z-7, S2 Z-3).
  - Floor ring: T02 at r37 / r28 down to the arm face. This leaves a 2x2 mm corner.
  - Fillet: T10 at r36 / r27 on the arm face. Its R2 corner forms the R2 fillet.
  - In window and channel sectors, the deeper T02 cuts remove the corner, so no fillet remains there, which matches the CAD.
- **Window.**
  - Rows Y50/58/66 at 8 mm stepover, then the offset loop at 5 mm from the wall, run CCW (climb).
  - Loop corners are r3 (R8−5) and boss arcs are r35/r26.
  - The loop points were checked to lie at the exact offset radius.
- **Outer channel.** One loop, CW (climb), on concentric offsets R120 / R26 / R55 / R35 through the offset tangent points. It makes a 10 mm slot, i.e. full radial engagement.
- **Boss 1 top with T03.**
  - Ring at r18 (covers r15–21).
  - Ring at r27.8 (covers r24.8–30.8).
  - Pin path: PCD arcs between pins plus a full r4.75 loop around each pin, giving a D3.5 pin exactly.
- **Bore, hole and keyway.**
  - Bore and hole: centre plunge, then full circles at r5 / r10, so no loose core.
  - Keyway: T03 passes at X39 and X41 (± 1 mm in the 8 mm slot), from inside the already-open bore. The end corners come out R3.
- **Step-downs.**
  - General removal: ≤ 3.9 mm.
  - Keyway and pins: 2 mm.
  - Radial engagement up to 100% in slots and channels, accepted at the approved 200 / 150 mm/min.
- **Tool fit.**
  - Deepest cut is 19.5 mm, below the 25 mm flute length of T02, T03 and T10.
  - Window R8 corners are larger than the T02 radius of 5 mm.
  - The keyway (8 mm) and the pin gap (6.25 mm) need T03 (D6).
  - The R2 fillets need the T10 corner radius R2.

## 5. Uncertainty and limitations

**Gate G1 – geometry**
- **CAD revision.** The configuration lists model_microversion `4d05edae…`, but the manifest has element_microversion `6e87bc46…`, and its "named_version" field holds the version id. Confirm both come from "Ch2-Final" before accepting any coordinate.
- **Coordinate transforms.** Please check these against a CAD overlay:
  - CAD to S1: X+40, Y+40, Z−14.
  - CAD to S2: X = 130 − Xc, Y = Yc + 40, Z = −10 − Zc.
  - Spot checks: larger boss at S1 (40,40) / S2 (130,40); keyway towards +Y (S1 Y54.457–58); pins at S1 (40,63) and (40,17).
- **Hand-computed offsets.** The offset tangent points, window loop and strip end points were computed by hand from the manifest arcs. Plot them against the part outline.
- **Missing fits and tolerances.** The D30 bore, D20 hole, keyway and pins have no tolerances. Each is cut in one pass to nominal size, with no finishing allowance and no reamer or boring tool. Real fits need an approved finishing method.
- **Keyway end corners.** The CAD corners are sharp, but T03 leaves R3. OP95 (broach / EDM / file) is outside the simulator.
- **R2 fillet extents.** The angular limits come from the contour and window tangent points, not from CAD. Check that no fillet is expected in the window or channel sectors.
- **Edge breaks and chamfers.** These are unknown, and none are programmed. T09 has no cutting data.
- **Raw faces left as finished.** Pin tops and boss bottoms are the raw block faces, with facing allowance 0. The real block thickness tolerance becomes the part tolerance.

**Gate G2 – process**
- **The waste frame is mostly not kept.** The plan assumed a frame for clamping, but the paths leave the 170×85 block:
  - S1 channel: the R120 arc reaches about Y92.8, beyond the back edge at Y85, between roughly X41 and X128.
  - Boss-2 wall and channel paths reach X176, beyond the right edge at X170.
  - Boss-1 rings (r42) cut past the front and left edges to Z−9.
  - After S1, the part therefore hangs on the 4.5 mm bottom skin, with only partial frame walls. Check clamping rigidity in S1 and the support-fixture design in S2.
- **Separation in OP180.** The part comes free in the last pass. The S2 fixture must hold it and keep the pins (S2 underside) clear. MillLab does not model this.
- **Full-width slotting.** The channel, strips and keyway cut at 100 % radial engagement, up to 3.9 mm deep at 200 mm/min (about 0.017 mm/tooth on T02). This is within the approved data, but low chip load can cause rubbing. Check with the team.
- **Plunges.** Vertical plunges at F60 assume T02, T03 and T10 are centre-cutting. T10 plunges into the 2×2 mm fillet stock at r36. Confirm this, or change to ramp entries.
- **Tools and coolant.** Only T02, T03 and T10 are used. T08 is excluded because of its open item. No coolant is programmed (not specified).

**Gate G3 – code**
- **Each setup's MillLab run starts from a raw block.** The S2 simulation therefore shows only the bottom-side cuts on a full block, and the finished part is never shown in a single run. Verify the result by combining both runs.
- **Run order matters.** The per-operation files are only valid in sequence (as in the consolidated programs). If run alone on a raw block:
  - OP70 plunges 7.9 mm into solid material.
  - OP90 plunges 10 mm into solid material.
  - OP130 and OP160 assume earlier operations have already cleared their start points.
- **Commands to test in MillLab.** Full circles programmed with identical start/end and I/J, modal F on arcs, G43 Hnn after M06, and M30 at the end of each operation file. Each of these still needs testing.
- **Arc rounding.** I/J values are rounded to 4 decimals, and radius mismatches are ≤ 0.001 mm. Confirm MillLab's arc tolerance accepts this.
- **Tool registers.** H/D entries must match the magazine (H02 = 75, H03 = 62, H10 = 80). The programs use no D registers, because all paths are tool-centre with G40.

Both setups are now complete. The most useful next step is the G1 overlay of the S1/S2 toolpaths on the Onshape outline, since every other check depends on those coordinates.
