## 3. Toolpath assumptions
- **Frame transform.** S1: Z = a − 210. S2: Z = −a. X is always the diameter. All NC coordinates are derived this way, with no other offsets.
- **Approach and retract.**
  - Approach point is X110 Z2 before every cut (2.5 mm radial clearance over the D105 bar).
  - Drills approach on the axis at X0 Z2 and retract to Z5.
  - Threading starts at Z10, X56 (3 mm radial clearance over D50).
  - S2 excess facing starts at Z31, reached along X110, so the tool stays radially outside the real Z+29 blank end.
  - Before every tool change: `G28 U0 W0`, then `M05`.
- **Facing (G94).**
  - Facing goes to X−1.6 (2 × nose radius past centre) so no pip remains.
  - S1 is one 1 mm pass.
  - S2 is 12 passes of 2.5 mm. Each pass starts 2 mm above the previous face, which keeps the feed retract of the cycle short.
- **OD roughing (G71 type I).**
  - 2.5 mm radial per pass, R1.0, U1.0 W0.5, F0.20.
  - Both profiles are monotonic in X, start with an X-only `G00` block and end with an exit to X106, above the stock.
  - The first passes from X110 are partly in air.
  - Roughing is uncompensated; the nose R0.8 error on the R5 arcs is covered by the allowance.
- **OD finishing (T02).**
  - One explicit contour per setup with G42 instead of G70. This keeps each op file self-contained and avoids G70/P-Q dependence across files.
  - The whole contour runs at 500 rpm / F0.10. The 800 rpm straight-pass data is not used, to avoid a speed change mid-contour.
  - Exit is G01 up the step face to X106, then a rapid at X110.
- **Bore D30 (T06).**
  - The G90 ID box cycle starts at X11 Z2, inside the D13 pilot and in front of the face.
  - Passes are 1 mm radial (X15 … X29). The return stroke of each box faces the bore bottom.
  - The finish pass with G41 runs down the wall, then across the bottom face to X12, which is 0.5 mm inside the D13 hole. Compensation is cancelled on the move to X11, then the tool retracts along the axis.
- **Drilling.**
  - A 2 mm spot is drilled first.
  - D13 is peck-drilled in 10 mm pecks to tip Z−69.
  - Re-entry is G00 inside the already-drilled hole, 1 mm short of the previous depth. The team should check this against the rule "G00 only outside material."
- **Thread.**
  - G92 radial infeed with decreasing depth, d_n = 3.067 · √(n/12), so the chip area stays roughly constant. First pass 0.885 mm radial, last 0.131 mm, plus one spring pass.
  - G97 S150, F = pitch 5.0.
  - Cycle end Z−120. At the major diameter the insert flank reaches about Z−122.2, leaving about 2.8 mm to the fillet tangent.
- **Why each tool fits.**
  - T01 (80°, R0.8): robust insert for facing and roughing, with approved data.
  - T02 (35° VNMG, 93°): its nose R0.4 is smaller than R5, so it can generate both concave fillets and finish the shoulders while moving +X.
  - T06 (D10 bar): the only boring bar with approved data, and the only one that can enter the D13 pilot. T12 has no data, and its 16 mm bar is larger than the 13 mm hole.
  - T04 (60°): matches the assumed 60° profile; its reach of 15 exceeds the 3.07 mm depth.
  - T10: the 2 mm spot depth is well within its reach of 10.
  - T05 (D13): equals the F17 diameter.
- **Safe zones.**
  - No motion goes beyond S1 Z−152 (the jaws start at Z−214).
  - No motion goes beyond S2 Z−60 below X106.

**G1 – geometry gate**
- **External thread F20.** Designation, depth, class and turned major-diameter tolerance are UNKNOWN. OP70's D50 and all OP80 values rest on assumption A10 (ISO 60° basic depth 3.067 mm, end at Z−120). Approve or replace these before OP80 is accepted.
- **Thread end.** The CAD thread runs to a=85, the fillet tangent (F22), with no runout or undercut. The CAD owner must decide the real thread end and whether a relief groove is wanted (T03 has no cutting data).
- **Internal thread F21 and bore F18.** The thread designation is UNKNOWN, and the bore diameter conflicts (Ø12 in the geometry, Ø14 in the Hole feature). Neither F03 (594.705 mm²) nor F19 closes arithmetically with either value. The data hint that Ø12 is a thread minor diameter, but that is unverified.
- **F22 interpretation.** I read the face at a=85 as the thread's end cap, not a shoulder. Please confirm.
- **Key envelope.** The spigot is turned to Ø36.7 because the key's corners need Ø36.68; the Ø29.9 spigot and the key itself are milled. Confirm this split with the route sheet (ops 230–250).
- **Missing specifications.** Tolerances, surface finish and chamfers are missing, so all edges are programmed sharp. The CAD material is null; C45 is taken from the configuration.

**G2 – process gate**
- **Internal features not producible with the configured tool table.**
  - The Ø13 bore needs 168.58 mm depth from S1, but T05 reaches 70; only a=~145–170 is drilled.
  - There is no tool for the Ø12 bore from solid.
  - T09 cannot cut the internal thread: its 12 mm bar does not fit a Ø12 bore, and its reach of 15 mm is less than 41.42 mm.
  - These are OP140–OP160, marked `simulator_supported: false`. The team decided not to change tools for reach, so this needs a decision: a longer drill, or a geometry or route change.
- **Setup 2 workholding.** Grip surface and length are unspecified. The programs assume a protected grip on Ø50 with nothing in front of S2 Z−82. If the Ø100 flange is gripped, the X106 exits at Z−60 may collide.
- **Setup 2 blank length.** The real part has 29 mm of excess beyond a=0, so OP110 faces it off in 12 passes. In LatheLab these are mostly air cuts. If the team saws to length instead, delete passes 1–11 and the Z31 approach.
- **Values I chose (not approved).** Facing axial depth 2.5 mm, G71 R1.0, 10 mm pecks, rapid re-entry into the drilled hole, drill tip at Z−69, U1.0/W0.5 interpretation of "0.5 per side", feeds 0.20/0.10/0.08, no coolant, M03 for all tools.
- **Tool reach vs shoulders.** The shoulders are 25 mm radial at a=80 and 15 mm at a=60, against a reach of 15 for T01/T02. Confirm what "reach" means for OD tools in LatheLab.
- **T06 in the pilot hole.** Check that T06 (10 mm bar) can enter and retract in the Ø13 pilot without its body touching the wall.
- **Drilled bore as finish.** Confirm whether the drilled Ø13 is acceptable as the finished bore.
